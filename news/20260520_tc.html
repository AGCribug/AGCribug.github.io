In-situ TEM study of hydride evolution in a zirconium alloy under H2+ irradiation

撰稿人：Shiping Hong

發布於 2026-05-20 08:11（UTC+8）

Zirconium alloys inevitably absorb hydrogen during reactor environment, leading to the precipitation of ZrHx that obviously degrade material mechanical integrity. However, the temperature-dependent mechanisms governing hydride nucleation and competitive growth under hydrogen pickup conditions remain unclear due to the lack of direct in-situ observations. In this work, H₂⁺ irradiation was employed to simulate hydrogen pickup under well-controlled conditions, and in-situ transmission electron microscopy (TEM) was used to directly observe the nucleation, growth and dissolution behavior of hydrides in a Zr-0.75 %Sn-0.5 %Nb-0.4 %Fe alloy at 20 °C and 125 °C. Temperature strongly influences ZrHx precipitation and growth. Hydrides show high nucleation density and limited growth, forming a fine distribution at 20 °C. Nucleation density decreases while growth is enhanced at 125 °C, with hydrides preferentially forming at GBs along the [1101] direction of the α-Zr matrix. Competitive growth between intragranular and GB hydrides is observed, and GBs limit hydride length. In-situ annealing shows progressive hydride dissolution with increasing temperature. Overall, hydride evolution is governed by a temperature-controlled transition in hydrogen transport mode, which dictates the balance between nucleation density, growth kinetics, and grain-boundary constraint.

DOI: 10.1016/j.jnucmat.2026.156753
